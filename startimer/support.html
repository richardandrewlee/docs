Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't I hear the counting voice?

Check that "Voice" is toggled on in Settings, your ringer/silent switch is off, and media volume is up. Speech uses your device's built-in voices — if a selected accent isn't installed, iOS may fall back to the default voice.

Does the timer keep running when my screen locks?

Yes — counting and beeps continue with the screen locked or while using other apps, as long as a timer is running.

How do I add more voice accents?

Accents come from iOS system voices. To install more, go to iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, and download the ones you'd like. They'll appear in StarTimer automatically.

What does BPM do?

BPM controls how fast the counting goes. 60 BPM = one count per second (normal). Raise it to count faster, lower it to count slower — or use the tap-tempo button to match your pace.

Is my data collected?

No. StarTimer collects nothing and works fully offline. See the Privacy Policy.
