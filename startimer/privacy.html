StarTimer Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

The short version: StarTimer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

StarTimer does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers of any kind. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.

Data Storage

Your settings and saved timer presets (such as interval lengths, voice preferences, and beep tones) are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Network Access

StarTimer functions entirely offline and makes no network requests.

Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
